How it works

How a job runs

  1. Pick an approved workload

    Choose from the list the admin trusts. Each one runs from a pinned container image.

  2. Set it up

    Rounds, batch size, seed, or your own CSV table. Every setting is checked before the job is queued.

  3. Watch it train

    The live log refreshes every 5 seconds, with a round counter and a loss chart.

  4. Keep the results

    Download metrics.json and the trained model.pt from the job page.

Limits

Why it's safe to share

Sealed containers

Jobs run with no network, read-only files, a non-root user, and memory and time limits.

Fair limits

Per-member limits keep the queue moving for everyone.

Honest numbers

The site never says a GPU job worked unless the job's own output proves it.
